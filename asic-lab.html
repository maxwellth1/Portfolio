A look at my digital systems design work in ECE337, with original architecture diagrams from two labs focused on serial communication and controller-driven datapath operations. These diagrams document how state machines coordinate control signals, counters, and datapath blocks.

Lab 05

UART Receiver Control & Timing

This lab breaks a UART receiver into coordinated control and timing logic. The RCU state-transition diagram maps the sequence of states as a packet is received, checked, and loaded; the RTL view shows how state logic and output logic translate that sequence into control signals. The timer diagram illustrates the counters and comparisons that generate timing strobes and packet-completion signals.

Lab 06

4-Point Controller & Datapath Sequencing

This lab focuses on a controller that sequences multi-cycle datapath operations. The flowchart lays out the control path through load, wait, shift, arithmetic, accumulation, and error-handling states. The RTL diagrams show the controller's next-state and output logic, plus the magnitude block's input/output interface. Together, they illustrate how a finite-state machine schedules operations and responds to conditions such as overflow.
